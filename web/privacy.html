Privacy Policy & Consent

♿ 本網站設計上遵循 WCAG 2.1 Level AA 無障礙標準。

📊 數據收集與使用 Data Collection

CoA-Agent 以你嘅私隱為最優先考量。我哋只會收集提供呢個服務所需嘅帳戶資料，例如你儲存嘅聯絡人同偏好設定；對話內容唔會以原文形式長期保存——只有喺你嘅照顧者提示功能入面，先會儲存一句好簡短嘅提示原因（例如「對日期感到混亂」），唔會儲存完整對話。

CoA-Agent treats your privacy as the top priority. We only collect the account data the service actually needs — such as saved contacts and preferences. Conversations are not kept verbatim; only the caregiver-alert feature stores a short reason (e.g. "confused about the date"), never a full conversation.

⚠️ 醫療免責聲明 Medical Disclaimer

CoA-Agent 並非醫療器材。所有回應僅供參考及支持用途，不能取代專業醫療建議、診斷或治療。如有即時危險或醫療緊急情況，請立即撥打 999 或當地緊急熱線，不要等候使用本應用程式。

CoA-Agent is not a medical device. All responses are for informational and supportive purposes only, and do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number now — do not wait to use this app.

🛡️ 你的權利 Your Rights

你可以隨時停止使用 CoA-Agent。如果你係照顧者，只有你連結咗嘅照顧對象嘅安全或認知狀況提示先會顯示俾你，唔會顯示日常對話內容。如果你想連結或者解除連結照顧對象，隨時可以喺照顧者模式入面處理。

You can stop using CoA-Agent at any time. If you're a caregiver, only safety or cognitive-status alerts for a patient you've linked to are ever shown to you — never their day-to-day conversation content. Linking or unlinking a care recipient can be done at any time from Caregiver Mode.

📁 數據存儲與跨境傳輸 Data Storage & Cross-Border Transfer

本系統所有數據存儲於香港本地/雲端伺服器，不會進行跨境轉移。

All data for this system is stored on local/cloud servers based in Hong Kong and will not be transferred across borders.

如對呢份政策有任何疑問，請電郵至 ageing@hku.hk。

If you have any questions about this policy, please email ageing@hku.hk.
